Simple by design.

Broad exposure to Indian and US equities through four index funds and one actively managed flexi-cap fund.

UTI Nifty Next 50 Index Fund 10%

Fund weights are not the same as underlying country exposure; PPFAS can hold international equities. A 100% equity portfolio is not appropriate for every investor.

Our philosophy

Peace of mind starts with a plan.

A practical approach to navigating two markets without predicting the next winner.

India + United States

Two markets. No crystal ball.

At different times, Indian and US equities take turns leading. Chasing whichever market has just performed best can turn long-term investing into a cycle of second-guessing.

Our starting point is a 50/50 target allocation to India-focused and US-focused funds , rather than a prediction about which country wins next. Because Parag Parikh Flexi Cap may own overseas stocks, actual underlying country exposure can differ.

Guessing whether it's a Safed कबूतर or वाइट Pigeon. Have a 50–50 US/India portfolio & be SafedPigeon! ↗
